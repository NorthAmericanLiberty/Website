How It Works

Live in about a week

Works with the number you already have

Customers keep calling the number on your truck and your cards. Calls you miss are forwarded to the system, which texts back from a local business number we set up for you, or from your own number if it can be moved over.

Texting done by the rules

The system only texts people who contacted your business first. Every message lets them reply STOP, and we register your business with the phone carriers as business texting requires. No mass marketing blasts.

You stay in control

You approve every message template. Replies come to one app on your phone, and you can take over any conversation at any time.

Questions

Before you ask

How long does setup take?

About one week. Registering your business for texting with the phone carriers can take several business days, so we start that on day one.

What do I need to do?

About 30 minutes with us: tell us how you handle calls and estimates, approve the message wording, and install one app on your phone.

Who writes the text messages?

We write them with you, in your words, and you approve every template before it goes live.

Can I cancel?

Yes. After the 3-month minimum, plans are month to month. Your customer list and messages stay yours.

What if it doesn't work for my business?
