About

Built by a former concrete contractor.

North American Liberty helps owners stop losing work to missed calls and forgotten follow-ups, in plain steps and at a fair price.

Richard Russell, Founder

Before North American Liberty, I owned and ran a residential concrete company. I did the estimating, pricing, proposals and change orders, coordinated crews and deliveries, and finished concrete myself. I found customers through referrals, Facebook and our Google listing, and I learned the hard way how many jobs disappear when nobody calls back.

Running that company taught me a simple lesson: the business that answers first, follows up and keeps its word wins the job.

Most marketing companies are built for big shops with big budgets. North American Liberty is built for owner-operators. We set things up for you, explain them plainly, charge fair prices, and you own everything we build.

Richard Russell
Founder, North American Liberty
