Questions

Common questions

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. Customers keep calling the number they already know. Calls you miss are forwarded to the system, which texts back from a local business number we set up for you, or from your own number if it can be moved over.

Is this legal to text people?

Yes, when it is done right. The system only texts people who contacted your business first, every message lets them reply STOP, and we register your business with the phone carriers as required for business texting.

Am I locked into a long contract?

Monthly plans have a 3-month minimum, then run month to month. You own your website, your Google listing and your customer list.

What does AI have to do with it?

AI helps draft replies, follow-ups and review responses so they sound like you. Nothing goes out that you have not approved, and you never have to learn new software.

Do you only work in Dallas–Fort Worth?

We are based in Dallas and do in-person setup across DFW. We also take clients in other areas by phone and video.
