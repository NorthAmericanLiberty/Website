Privacy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Information you send us

When you request a quote, you give us your name, phone number, city, the type of job and any details you add. The form is delivered to us through Formspree, a form service. We use this information only to contact you about your job. We do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Analytics cookies

If you click Accept on our cookie notice, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site, such as which pages are viewed, how people found us, the type of device and the general area a visit comes from. It does not tell us who you are.

If you click Decline, or never choose, analytics stays off. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of the home page. Your choice is saved in your browser.

Fonts

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. To deliver them, Google receives your browser’s IP address. No cookies are set for this.
